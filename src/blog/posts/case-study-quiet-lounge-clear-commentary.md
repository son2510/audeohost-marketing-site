Not every venue wants a loud room. Some must keep volume low due to local rules. Others protect a premium atmosphere.

Yet customers still want the match. They want commentary and context. Therefore, the challenge becomes clear.

## Starting point

The venue had a quiet lounge area with screens. The customer base included sports fans and casual guests.

Turning up commentary damaged the vibe. Turning it off reduced engagement. As a result, staff avoided the issue.

## Goal

The venue wanted a calm room and fewer complaints. It also wanted better match retention. The solution needed to feel natural.

## What changed

The venue offered a phone-based listening option for those who wanted it. That kept speakers low while fans stayed informed.

**What surprised the team:** Casual guests appreciated the calmer room. Sports fans appreciated the choice.

## Why it worked

The experience matched expectations. Quiet meant quiet. However, it did not mean “miss the game.”

Staff also gained control. They stopped micromanaging sound settings. Consequently, service became smoother.

## Bonus: the venue promoted its next events

Once customers used their phones in-venue, reminders became easier. The venue highlighted upcoming fixtures and in-house events.

That improved repeat visits. It also made the lounge feel active throughout the week. Small wins added up.
