The real ROI argument

You already paid for the game. And the band. Most nights, that spend is invisible.

Broadcast packages are a fixed monthly or per-event cost, whether three fans watch or three hundred. Musicians and comedians get paid the same whether the room's half-empty or rammed. Most venues only manage to activate that spend on the one day it barely needs the help.

Saturday, 3pm kick-off

Already full

The room's busy either way — word of mouth, habit, a big fixture. Audeohost adds atmosphere here, not attendance. Nice, but it isn't where the growth is.

Tuesday, 2pm kick-off

Already paid for. Empty anyway.

Three fans, house music, a screen nobody can actually hear. Same broadcast line-item as Saturday. Zero extra cost to give those three real commentary — and a reason for the next three to start showing up too.

You can't fill an already-full room. You can turn the nights it's empty into a reason to come back — using sport and entertainment you've already paid for, no matter what night it falls on.

FAQ

Before you call

Do customers need an app?

Yes. Audeohost-Events (events & sound) and Audeohost-Live (sound only) are both free on the App Store and Google Play. Guests scan a table QR code and are listening in seconds.

How long does setup take?

The Audeo-Portal can be configured in under two hours. Audeo-Server may need a short on-site visit to confirm Wi-Fi, cabling and screen layout.

Can we keep house speakers low but still offer commentary?

Yes. Guests hear full commentary in the app while your house volume stays wherever your policy sets it.

Can different tables listen to different matches?

Yes. Each guest picks their own audio feed in the app, so multiple games run side-by-side without commentary clashing.

Do we need special hardware?

Audeo-Server uses a small on-site device. We confirm exactly what's needed during the site survey and keep the install as compact as possible.

Does the audio work outside the venue?

No — by design. Audio is delivered over your venue's own Wi-Fi, never over cellular and never off-premises. That keeps it a genuine in-venue perk, not something guests get for free from the car park.

Does this help hard-of-hearing guests?

Yes. Because audio goes straight to a guest's own phone and their own earbuds, it's functionally a personal hearing aid in a loud bar or a large auditorium — clearer than straining to hear a house speaker from the back of the room.

Can Audeohost connect to our POS or ticketing system?

Tell us what you need. We'll talk through pulling menu and specials data from your POS, or handing off to your ticketing system so a guest can book a seat for a bigger show — the right integration depends on what you're already running.
